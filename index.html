04 — Margins & cash

Profit and cashpart ways.

Margins first: EBITDA against PAT, every fiscal year through FY2028. Then the cash story that made this page stricter — the year TCS stopped publishing a free cash flow line.

Reading the FY2026 dip

Both margins dip into FY2026 on the plotted (statutory) basis: PAT lands at 18.5% of revenue and EBITDA near 27.1%, because TCS booked roughly ₹3,362 Cr of one-off items in the year.

Excluding those items, the company's own framing is 25.0% operating margin (+70 bps) and 19.8% net margin. The chart keeps the statutory basis on purpose — the book that re-runs itself should not pick the flattering line.

The empty FY2026 slot

For FY2026 the cash bar is empty on purpose: TCS reported ₹52,094 Cr of operating cash flow, but no free cash flow line — its FY2026 disclosures define cash differently, and the honest slot is an absent one, not a guessed number.

FY2027 opens in your selected case color, with the model's own FCF — operations less capex, per the workbook rules. Hover any bar for the exact pair and the FCF/PAT conversion.

06 — Method & sources

How this pagewas built.

It started as a Power BI dashboard; this page is its web edition — rebuilt as a single interactive document with the same discipline. No charting library, no template, no generated filler.

The build, on the record

  1. Freeze the inputs

    The source workbook (FY2018–FY2025, 11 metrics; 10 assumption rows × 3 cases) is pinned by SHA-256 inside data.js, so any future edit is a deliberate one.

  2. Re-key and verify

    Every cell was re-keyed by hand into the data module, then script-checked against the workbook — 88/88 historical cells and 30/30 case values match exactly.

  3. Pin FY2026 to the filing

    The FY2026 actuals come straight from TCS's Q4/FY2026 results of 9 April 2026 — revenue ₹2,67,021 Cr, statutory PAT ₹49,454 Cr, EPS ₹136.01. Where the model's proxy differs (EBITDA), the definition sits next to the number.

  4. Draw the charts by hand

    Eight chart kinds — line, area, bar, paired bars, margin lines, spread, comparison and sparklines — are written directly as SVG paths and rects in two small files. Hover, focus and crosshair behavior included.

  5. Make the state honest

    One shared state drives everything: the hero deck, KPI cards, both bar charts, the comparison, assumptions and the ledger. A live region announces each change to screen readers, and the CSV export hands you whatever view you have selected.

Made by hand

This page was built by a human, start to finish — no site generator, no chart library, no auto-filled copy. The slow parts (re-keying 118 values, verifying nine years of statements, drawing each chart as raw SVG) were done deliberately.
